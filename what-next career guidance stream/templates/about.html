About What Next?

Guiding students toward careers that truly fit them.

Choosing a career path after school can feel overwhelming. There are hundreds of courses, thousands of colleges, and countless job titles — and very little guidance on how they connect.

What Next? is a simple career guidance platform built to help students understand their own interests and match them to realistic courses, colleges, jobs, and a step-by-step roadmap. It's not about telling you what to do — it's about giving you a clearer starting point.

What We Offer

  • A quick, free interest-based aptitude test
  • Personalized career category recommendations
  • Curated course and college suggestions
  • Realistic job role examples for each path
  • A step-by-step roadmap from school to career

Our Mission

We believe every student deserves clarity, not just information overload. Our goal is to make career exploration simple, encouraging, and genuinely useful.
